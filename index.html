About Gulu University Journal

Journal Overview

Gulu University Journal is an international, peer-reviewed, open-access journal dedicated to publishing high-quality research across all academic disciplines. Established in 2010, our journal has grown to become a respected platform for researchers worldwide to share their findings with the global academic community.

Our mission is to advance knowledge and foster innovation by providing a rigorous yet efficient publication process that maintains the highest standards of academic excellence while promoting accessibility and sustainability in scholarly communication.

Scope

Gulu University Journal welcomes submissions from all fields of study, including but not limited to:

  • Natural Sciences
  • Engineering and Technology
  • Medical and Health Sciences
  • Agricultural Sciences
  • Social Sciences
  • Humanities
  • Interdisciplinary Studies

Open Access Policy

Gulu University Journal is committed to the principles of open access. All articles published in our journal are immediately and permanently available online for anyone to read, download, and share, free of charge. We believe this approach maximizes the visibility, readership, and impact of the research we publish.

Peer Review Process

All submissions undergo a rigorous double-blind peer review process:

  1. Initial editorial screening for scope and quality
  2. Assignment to at least two independent experts in the field
  3. Double-blind review process (typically 4-6 weeks)
  4. Editorial decision based on reviewer recommendations
  5. Opportunity for revision if required

Indexing and Metrics

Editorial Policies

Gulu University Journal adheres to the highest standards of publication ethics and follows the guidelines established by the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE).

Authorship

All listed authors must have made significant contributions to the research and approved the final version of the manuscript.

Originality

Submissions must represent original work that has not been published previously and is not under consideration elsewhere.

Plagiarism

All submissions are screened for plagiarism. Manuscripts with unacceptable levels of similarity to other works will be rejected.

Publication Ethics

Responsibilities of Editors

Editors evaluate manuscripts based on intellectual content without regard to race, gender, sexual orientation, religious belief, ethnic origin, citizenship, or political philosophy.

Responsibilities of Reviewers

Reviewers must maintain confidentiality of the review process and declare any conflicts of interest.

Responsibilities of Authors

Authors must present accurate results and discussion, provide proper attribution, and disclose any conflicts of interest.

Submission Guidelines

Manuscript Preparation

All submissions must be prepared according to the following guidelines:

Structure

Research articles should generally include the following sections:

  • Title page with author information
  • Abstract (structured recommended)
  • Keywords (3-6 terms)
  • Introduction
  • Materials and Methods
  • Results
  • Discussion
  • Conclusions
  • Acknowledgments (if applicable)
  • References
  • Figures and Tables with captions

Formatting

  • 12-point Times New Roman font
  • Double-spaced text
  • 1-inch (2.5 cm) margins on all sides
  • Continuous line numbering
  • Page numbers in footer

References

References should follow APA (7th edition) style. Examples:

Journal Article:

Author, A. A., & Author, B. B. (Year). Title of article. Journal Name, volume(issue), page range. https://doi.org/xxxx

Book:

Author, A. A. (Year). Title of work. Publisher.

Website:

Author, A. A. (Year, Month Day). Title of page. Site Name. URL

Figures and Tables

  • High-resolution (minimum 300 dpi) figures in TIFF, EPS, or PDF format
  • Figures should be submitted as separate files in addition to being embedded in the manuscript
  • Tables should be editable (created in Word, not as images)
  • All figures and tables must be cited in the text

Supplementary Materials

Additional files (datasets, code, extended methods, etc.) can be submitted as supplementary materials. These will be published online alongside the article.
